## Fidelity and acceptance boundaries

The original manual informed the dry opening, piece behavior, hazards, and scoring relationships. Continuous alignment with area-weighted terrain edits is an adaptation: the original analog placement resolution and terrain-edit math have not been measured. The underlying fluid field still has 32 × 32 samples. Shapes, timings, scoring values, lake/duck/rainbow thresholds, fluid simulation, quake damage, and bounded re-bomb cascades are independently tuned. Fire evaporation applies to simulation at impact rather than progressively burning through a lake; a short visual water fade and steam bridge the transition. Camera turning is now implemented; camera zoom, Pro/mystery pieces, original lessons/challenges/handicaps, and multiplayer remain absent.

Physical-phone frame rate, thumb comfort, browser-specific home-screen installation, subjective audio mix, and long-session difficulty still need hands-on acceptance. Touch emulation does not establish those. Visibility auto-pause is implemented, but the automated browser did not expose a reliable hidden-tab transition, so that behavior is not claimed as verified.

The [art notes](ART.md) contain generated assets' paths, prompts, and provenance, including the retired ground texture. QA scripts and screenshots remain in ignored `output/playwright/`: `v7-checks.js`, `v7-production.js`, `v7-feedback.js`, `v7-audio.js`, `v6-graphics.js`, `v5-motion.js`, `v4-resilience.js`, and `v7-live.js`. Earlier scripts are historical.
